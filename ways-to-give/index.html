Your Gift Transforms Lives

Access to safe, essential, and timely surgical care saves lives and restores communities. It also creates an opportunity for partnership and education between medical volunteers and local professionals. Help us bring surgical care to those who need it most.

Consider a Monthly Donation

Monthly donations help strengthen our year-round programming. Give below and see the life-transforming care your gift could provide.

Choose an amount and give safely online.

Medical supplies for one patient surgery

There Are Many Ways to Give

Gift Stock

Solidarity Bridge can receive your stock gift through our brokerage account.

Matching Gifts

Double your donation with a matching gift from your employer — ask if your company offers a matching gift program.

Legacy Gifting

Make an estate gift to ensure future access to critical healthcare by naming Solidarity Bridge in your will or other charitable vehicle.

Dedicate a Gift

Give a gift in someone's honor or in support of a medical volunteer, and we'll notify the honoree on your behalf.

Donor Advised Fund

Already set aside money for charitable giving? Consider a designation to Solidarity Bridge through your DAF.

Sponsor a Trip

Help sponsor a surgical volunteer team traveling to Bolivia or Paraguay and extend the reach of our medical missions.
