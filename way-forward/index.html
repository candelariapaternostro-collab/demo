Your Gift Is Shaping the Negev's Future Today

Together, we build something stronger — resilience, hope, and community.

For more than fifty years, Ben-Gurion University has stood at the center of the Negev, driving the region's economic and social growth. At this pivotal moment, BGU's part in rebuilding the region matters more than ever — and the Negev's path forward depends on the security and hope the university helps provide.

To meet this moment, we've launched a global effort to raise $1 billion for new campus infrastructure, broader student access, and world-class research. Reaching this goal will cement BGU's place as a hub of innovation — strengthening the Negev, reinforcing Israel, and extending its discoveries around the world. Join us on the Way Forward.

The campaign runs from October 1, 2020 through September 30, 2030.

Americans for Ben-Gurion University is a nonprofit organization supporting Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel's youngest, most innovative university.
