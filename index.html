Increasing access to surgery

We believe that healthcare is a human right.

In the spirit of solidarity, we partner with communities in Bolivia and Paraguay to increase access to safe, affordable surgery and other essential healthcare.

Millions of people across Bolivia and Paraguay still lack access to affordable, timely surgical care.
