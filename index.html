Partners in the Remarkable

A4BGU exists to help Ben-Gurion University of the Negev drive economic growth and social diversity across the Negev — a region vital to Israel's long-term strength. When you start a fundraising page, you're inviting your own circle to become partners in that mission, one gift at a time.
